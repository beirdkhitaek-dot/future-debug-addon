Converted from the supplied WaterClient JAR:
- com.water.module.modules.donut.FutureDebug
- com.water.module.modules.render.FutureDebug

The original classes depend on com.water.module.Module, com.water.setting.Setting,
com.water.utils.RenderUtils and WaterClient-specific mappings. Those dependencies are
intentionally not copied.

The Meteor addon uses Meteor Module, Setting, Render3DEvent and native Minecraft chunk
APIs instead. Behavior is intended to match the debug scanner/rendering concept, not to
preserve WaterClient bytecode or its unrelated client code.
